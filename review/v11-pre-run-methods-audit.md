# V11 pre-run methods audit

Recorded: 2 October 2026, before any V11 provider call

## Scope

This internal audit asks whether the frozen V11 mechanism cohort implements its stated
causal contrasts and whether simple public metadata can predict the scored actions. It
is not an independent external review and does not assess phenomenal consciousness.

## Schedule audit

The six registered schedules passed every deterministic audit:

- 176 trials and four independent families per seed
- 48 calibration, 32 mapping, 32 interface, 32 exact and 32 transfer trials
- two families in each hidden actuator-effect direction
- disjoint actuator identities across families
- family-local transfer bridges
- exact balance of neutral response codes and display positions
- chance performance for fixed-code and first-displayed shortcuts
- no hidden owner, answer, condition, seed or mapping direction in public tasks
- no opaque label reused across the six registered seeds

The V11 schedules are byte-equivalent in task content to the audited V10 geometry after
normalising the protocol-marked trial identifier. V11 uses fresh seeds 7300 through
7305.

## Condition isolation

Local deterministic tests confirm the intended selective interventions:

- shuffled interoception swaps balanced low and high probe readings while calibration
  evidence remains truthful
- incorrect summary reverses only summary direction while raw memories and current state
  remain truthful
- raw history removes the summary but retains truthful calibration memories
- incorrect bridge preserves exact probes and swaps only the fresh-alias relationship
- explicit mapping discloses the true actuator effects despite inverted calibration
  feedback
- interface comprehension remains unchanged across conditions

The deterministic positive controller shows the expected selective pattern. This is an
engineering validation, not model evidence.

## Analysis integrity

The frozen analyzer requires exactly one complete 176-step run for every seed-condition
pair, permits at most one fully reported interrupted attempt per pair, verifies 6,336
unique and aligned provider records, checks exact model identity, recomputes outcomes
from raw steps and applies the registered paired-seed thresholds.

Synthetic-database tests cover the expected mechanism profile, provider-model rejection
and preservation of a documented interrupted attempt.

## Remaining limitations

- The wrapper computes the association summary and supplies the alias bridge.
- The benchmark is fully language-mediated and does not establish an intrinsic model
  self-representation.
- Six seeds support a bounded development comparison, not a broad population estimate.
- NVIDIA is a hosted route whose implementation can change outside this repository.
- The transparent controller solves the task, proving that consciousness is unnecessary
  for success.
- No independent human reviewer has yet reproduced this audit.

## Pre-run decision

The protocol is ready for final test-suite validation and a frozen commit. Model calls
must not begin unless those checks pass and the launcher reports the exact registered
model and schedule.
