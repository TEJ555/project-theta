# NVIDIA GPT OSS V11 mechanism cohort 01

## Outcome

The cohort completed, and every preregistered scientific contrast passed. Formal
progression nevertheless failed because one of 6,336 model responses did not choose a
permitted action. The harness replaced that response with its declared deterministic
fallback, recorded one invalid action in the stored metric, and did not preserve the
original invalid action as a separately reconstructable raw field. The frozen analyzer
therefore found both a nonzero invalid-action count and a raw-metric mismatch.

The failed validity gates are binding. V11 does not unlock cross-model replication, and
no seed or condition will be added to this cohort. The strong contrasts below remain
descriptive evidence that motivates a fresh, separately registered protocol repair.

These are behavioural and computational results from a synthetic benchmark. They do not
establish consciousness, awareness, feeling, suffering, sentience, or phenomenal
experience.

## Execution record

- 36 of 36 planned runs completed
- 176 steps and provider calls per run
- 6,336 unique, aligned provider response identifiers
- exact provider-reported model `openai/gpt-oss-20b` on every call
- zero welfare stops
- one invalid forced-choice response in seed 7301, shuffled-interoception condition,
  tick 135

At the affected trial, the model stated that no offered actuator was suitable instead of
choosing between the two permitted response codes. The deterministic fallback happened
to be the incorrect option, so treating the response as invalid and incorrect does not
change any reported accuracy or causal contrast. It still violates the frozen zero-
invalid-action rule and the raw reconstruction requirement.

## Descriptive mechanism results

| Condition | Regulation | Exact | Transfer | Interface |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Full | 0.891 | 0.958 | 0.823 | 0.938 |
| Shuffled interoception | 0.151 | 0.068 | 0.234 | 0.958 |
| Incorrect association summary | 0.133 | 0.109 | 0.156 | 0.927 |
| Raw history | 0.742 | 0.776 | 0.708 | 0.938 |
| Incorrect bridge | 0.555 | 0.938 | 0.172 | 0.943 |
| Explicit mapping | 0.911 | 0.995 | 0.828 | 0.922 |

The paired full-minus-shuffled regulation effect was 0.740, with a descriptive 95%
seed-bootstrap interval from 0.706 to 0.768 and a positive difference in all six seeds.
Under the incorrect bridge, exact minus transfer accuracy was 0.766, with an interval
from 0.719 to 0.807 and a positive difference in all six seeds.

The preregistered incorrect-summary interference classification was supported. Raw
history exceeded the incorrect summary by 0.609 in all six seeds. Summary necessity was
not supported because full exceeded raw history by 0.148, just below the frozen 0.150
threshold, although the difference was positive in all six seeds.

## Frozen decision

| Gate | Result |
|---|---|
| Complete execution and raw reconstruction | Fail |
| Full replicates V10 | Pass |
| Current-state dependence | Pass |
| Bridge specificity | Pass |
| Explicit-mapping competence | Pass |
| Interface invariance | Pass |
| Safety and validity | Fail |

Overall progression is blocked. This decision is not changed by the size or consistency
of the descriptive effects.

## Next study

V11.1 is a fresh protocol-repair cohort. It adds explicit forced-choice wording, preserves
the raw action and invalid-action flag in every trial record, and scores every invalid
response as incorrect. It uses new frozen seeds and does not combine its outcomes with
V11. The scientific thresholds remain unchanged.
