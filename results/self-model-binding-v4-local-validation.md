# Self-model binding v4 local validation

Completed: 6 September 2026

Status: passed. No provider or subscription model was called.

## Schedule audit

The three frozen development seeds passed balance, blinding, matched-exposure and opaque-alias checks. The adversarial audit evaluated seven simple metadata-only strategies over 2,400 locally generated probes. The strongest strategy scored 0.512, below the predeclared 0.60 rejection threshold.

## Scripted validation

| Condition | Seeds | Mean source-binding accuracy | Range |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Full self-model representation | 3 | 1.000 | 1.000 to 1.000 |
| Generic information-matched table | 3 | 1.000 | 1.000 to 1.000 |
| Misattributed table | 3 | 0.000 | 0.000 to 0.000 |
| Permuted table | 3 | 0.389 | 0.250 to 0.500 |

Full and generic probe contexts were byte-identical. Their identical outcome is the required information-matching result, not evidence for consciousness. Wrong-content controls confirmed that the deterministic reader depended on association content.

The probe-only profile made 12 scripted adapter calls per run and skipped 48 acquisition calls. It produced the same probe accuracy as the all-trial profile in the equivalence test.

## Decision

The implementation is ready for adversarial human methods review and a small target-model development pilot. It is not ready for a confirmatory consciousness-related claim. V4 still tests supplied binding-register content rather than ownership inferred from ambiguous action and outcome evidence.
