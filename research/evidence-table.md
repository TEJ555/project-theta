# Project Theta evidence table

## How to read this table

The table is an evidence map, not a league table of consciousness theories. A source
can support the study of a mechanism without showing that the mechanism is necessary
or sufficient for phenomenal consciousness. Project Theta uses the following labels:

- B: behavioural indicator, measured from actions or outputs
- C: computational indicator, verified in the implemented causal organization
- P: phenomenal consciousness, which Project Theta does not measure

## Field-level reviews and methods

| Source | Status and type | Main contribution | Important limit | Relevance to Theta |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Seth and Bayne 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41583-022-00587-4) | Peer-reviewed review | Compares major theory families and their empirical programs. | No theory is established as the agreed account. | Justifies a plural, theory-derived indicator battery. |
| [Mudrik et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neubiorev.2025.106053) | Peer-reviewed review | Authors from competing programs identify shared problems and disagreements. | Synthesis does not settle necessary or sufficient conditions. | Supports explicit uncertainty and cross-theory tests. |
| [Yaron et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-021-01284-5) | Peer-reviewed evidence database | Analysis of 412 experiments found theory support associated with methodological choices and frequent post hoc interpretation. | Coding published studies cannot remove publication or design bias. | Requires preregistration, matched controls, and null-result reporting. |
| [Cogitate Consortium et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08888-1) | Preregistered, multi-method adversarial test | A 256-person fMRI, MEG, and intracranial EEG study found mixed results and challenged key predictions of GNWT and IIT. | A single adversarial collaboration is not a final verdict on either family. | Warns against treating any one theory as a detector. |
| [Graziano et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2116933119) | Peer-reviewed conceptual framework | Separates questions and target phenomena that theories often conflate. | A framework organizes claims but does not validate a mechanism. | Supports separate outcomes for access, self-model, report, and state. |
| [Doerig et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1080/17588928.2020.1772214) | Peer-reviewed methodological proposal | Proposes hard criteria for empirical consciousness theories. | The criteria remain contested. | Encourages risky predictions and architecture-level ablations. |
| [Kleiner and Hoel 2021](https://doi.org/10.1093/nc/niab001) | Peer-reviewed methodology | Examines what falsification can mean for consciousness theories. | Auxiliary assumptions make clean falsification difficult. | Requires recording which assumptions connect each test to a theory. |
| [Del Pin et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1093/nc/niab019) | Peer-reviewed methodology | Sets out reasons and methods for comparing theories directly. | Theory comparison still depends on construct choices. | Supports matched, discriminating interventions rather than isolated demonstrations. |

## Global workspace and recurrent processing

| Source | Status and type | Main contribution | Important limit | Relevance to Theta |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Dehaene and Naccache 2001](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0010-0277(00)00123-2) | Peer-reviewed theory review | Describes conscious access as global availability through a workspace. | It is a neurocognitive theory, not a generic software checklist. | Motivates capacity-limited broadcast and cross-module availability as C indicators. |
| [Dehaene et al. 1998](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.95.24.14529) | Peer-reviewed computational model | Models a neuronal workspace for effortful tasks. | Task success in a model does not establish experience. | Shows that workspace claims can yield implemented, inspectable mechanisms. |
| [Mashour et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2020.01.026) | Peer-reviewed review | Updates GNW mechanisms and evidence for conscious processing. | Most evidence concerns biological brains and conscious access. | Guides broadcast, ignition-like competition, and flexible-use tests without claiming biological GNW. |
| [Shea and Frith 2019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2019.04.007) | Peer-reviewed theory argument | Argues that a global workspace account needs metacognitive representation. | The proposed relationship is disputed. | Supports testing workspace and self-monitoring jointly and separately. |
| [Whyte and Smith 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pneurobio.2020.101918) | Peer-reviewed formal model | Connects predictive processing with a global neuronal workspace. | Formal compatibility is not proof of the combined theory. | Motivates recurrent prediction and broadcast interactions. |
| [Lamme 2006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2006.09.001) | Peer-reviewed theory review | Makes local recurrent processing central to visual consciousness. | Evidence is tied to neural visual processing and remains debated. | Motivates recurrence ablation while keeping task information constant. |

## Higher-order representation and metacognition

| Source | Status and type | Main contribution | Important limit | Relevance to Theta |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Brown, Lau, and LeDoux 2019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2019.06.009) | Peer-reviewed review | Clarifies higher-order approaches and their variants. | Higher-order representation is not equivalent to fluent self-description. | Motivates causal self-model tests, not keyword scoring. |
| [Lau and Rosenthal 2011](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2011.05.009) | Peer-reviewed review | Relates empirical findings to higher-order awareness. | The evidence permits rival explanations. | Supports confidence and source-monitoring measures with controls. |
| [Fleming and Lau 2014](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2014.00443) | Peer-reviewed measurement review | Distinguishes measures of metacognitive sensitivity and bias. | Metacognitive performance is not itself phenomenal consciousness. | Requires calibration metrics rather than raw confidence alone. |
| [Fleming 2020](https://doi.org/10.1093/nc/niz020) | Peer-reviewed computational theory | Models awareness as inference in higher-order state space. | A formal model needs empirical and cross-system validation. | Motivates predictions about the agent's own states and errors. |

## Integrated information

| Source | Status and type | Main contribution | Important limit | Relevance to Theta |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Tononi 2004](https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2202-5-42) | Peer-reviewed theory | Introduces information integration as central to consciousness. | Early formulations do not provide a simple valid AI test. | Inspires causal integration questions only. |
| [Tononi et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1038/nrn.2016.44) | Peer-reviewed review | Connects IIT postulates to proposed physical mechanisms. | Biological and physical claims cannot be replaced by module counts. | Theta must not label ordinary software connectivity as Phi. |
| [Albantakis et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011465) | Peer-reviewed formal theory | Presents IIT 4.0 and its formal quantities. | Exact analysis is computationally demanding and theory-dependent. | Confirms that Theta currently has no valid IIT implementation. |
| [Bayne 2018](https://doi.org/10.1093/nc/niy007) | Peer-reviewed criticism | Questions the axiomatic foundations of IIT. | Philosophical criticism does not by itself refute all empirical work. | Requires presenting IIT inspiration and objections together. |
| [Doerig et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.concog.2019.04.002) | Peer-reviewed criticism | Uses the unfolding argument against causal-structure theories. | Defenders reject parts of the argument and its target assumptions. | Shows why input-output equivalence cannot verify internal consciousness claims. |
| [Merker, Williford, and Rudrauf 2022](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0140525X21002387) | Peer-reviewed target article with commentaries | Develops broad objections to IIT's identity claim and implications. | The debate contains many opposing commentaries. | Prevents using a single integration score as a consciousness score. |

## Predictive processing, embodiment, and interoception

| Source | Status and type | Main contribution | Important limit | Relevance to Theta |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Seth 2013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2013.09.007) | Peer-reviewed theory review | Links interoceptive inference, emotion, and embodied selfhood. | A synthetic numeric channel is not biological interoception or feeling. | Motivates a private, noisy body signal and prospective prediction tests. |
| [Barrett and Simmons 2015](https://doi.org/10.1038/nrn3950) | Peer-reviewed review | Develops an anatomical and predictive account of interoception. | Evidence concerns biological neural systems. | Supports separating hidden body state, noisy sensation, and action. |
| [Seth and Tsakiris 2018](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2018.08.008) | Peer-reviewed review | Argues that embodied physiological regulation contributes to selfhood. | It does not show that embodiment is sufficient for experience. | Motivates persistent body dynamics and body ablation. |
| [Hohwy 2012](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2012.00096) | Peer-reviewed theory | Relates attention and conscious perception to hypothesis testing. | Predictive processing covers many architectures and levels. | Motivates explicit predictions and errors, not generic next-token prediction claims. |
| [Whyte, Hohwy, and Smith 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.crneur.2022.100036) | Peer-reviewed formal model | Models conscious access through active inference and action selection. | Model fit does not distinguish phenomenal consciousness. | Supports testing whether internal predictions causally improve control. |
| [Garfinkel et al. 2015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biopsycho.2014.11.004) | Peer-reviewed empirical methods | Separates interoceptive accuracy, sensibility, and awareness. | Human heartbeat measures do not transfer directly to AI. | Supports separate objective performance and self-assessment metrics. |

## Attention schema

| Source | Status and type | Main contribution | Important limit | Relevance to Theta |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Graziano and Webb 2015](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2015.00500) | Peer-reviewed theory | Proposes an internal model of attention used for control and awareness attribution. | A descriptive attention model is not established as sufficient for experience. | Motivates an inspectable self-model of selected workspace contents. |
| [Wilterson et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pneurobio.2020.101844) | Peer-reviewed review | Reviews attention control through attention schema theory. | Evidence is compatible with other control accounts. | Requires a control benefit and matched no-schema condition. |
| [Wilterson and Graziano 2021](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2102421118) | Peer-reviewed computational experiment | A neural network agent used a descriptive attention model to improve attention control. | The study tests control, not phenomenal consciousness. | Direct precedent for classifying the result as C and B, never P. |

## Measurement and report confounds

| Source | Status and type | Main contribution | Important limit | Relevance to Theta |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Tsuchiya et al. 2015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2015.10.002) | Peer-reviewed methods review | Argues that report can confound consciousness with task and post-perceptual processing. | No-report measures require their own inference assumptions. | Self-report is logged as behaviour and paired with causal and task evidence. |
| [Duman et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2022.861517) | Peer-reviewed methodological criticism | Identifies alternative interpretations and limitations of no-report paradigms. | The critique does not rehabilitate uncritical report. | Prevents treating either report or no-report performance as decisive. |
| [Schlicht 2018](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.concog.2018.11.002) | Peer-reviewed methodology | Describes the dilemma between report dependence and uncertain proxies. | The dilemma has no simple technical solution. | Requires triangulation and modest inference. |
| [Cohen et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.0120-20.2020) | Peer-reviewed empirical study | Separates perceptual awareness from post-perceptual processing in a no-report design. | Neural findings in humans do not establish AI markers. | Motivates delayed probes and controls for report-production processes. |

## Artificial consciousness and responsible research

| Source | Status and type | Main contribution | Important limit | Relevance to Theta |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Dehaene, Lau, and Kouider 2017](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aan8871) | Peer-reviewed review | Separates unconscious computation, global access, and self-monitoring for machine comparison. | Functional categories do not settle phenomenology. | Supports distinct workspace and self-monitoring tests. |
| [Reggia 2013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neunet.2013.03.011) | Peer-reviewed review | Surveys computational models across workspace, integration, self-model, higher-order, and attention approaches. | Reports no compelling demonstration of phenomenal machine consciousness. | Supports computational modeling while preserving the P boundary. |
| [Gamez 2008](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.concog.2007.04.005) | Peer-reviewed review | Surveys progress and methodological problems in machine consciousness. | The field and systems have changed substantially since publication. | Provides historical context and recurring validation problems. |
| [Butlin et al. 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08708) | Scholarly report, preprint | Derives computational indicator properties from several consciousness theories. | Indicators inherit uncertainty from the theories and their transfer to AI. | Main precedent for Theta's theory-derived indicator approach. |
| [Butlin et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2025.10.011) | Peer-reviewed methods review | Refines the theory-derived indicator method for assessing AI systems. | Indicator satisfaction changes uncertainty but does not prove consciousness. | Requires system-specific internal evidence and theory pluralism. |
| [Chalmers 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.07103) | Scholarly preprint | Assesses current and future LLMs against proposed consciousness-relevant features. | Conclusions depend on disputed background assumptions and architecture knowledge. | Motivates examining persistent agency and recurrence outside the base model. |
| [Spasokukotskiy et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.3389/frobt.2024.1437496) | Peer-reviewed architecture proposal | Proposes a modular synthetic consciousness architecture. | Architecture labels do not validate consciousness or the modules' causal roles. | Useful as design comparison, not evidence that Theta creates consciousness. |
| [Long et al. 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00986) | Research report, preprint | Argues for preparation under uncertainty about AI consciousness and robust agency. | Normative recommendations are not empirical evidence of current AI welfare. | Supports low-cost precautions, assessment, and governance planning. |
| [Butlin and Lappas 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.07290) | Research principles, preprint | Proposes public principles for objectives, procedures, knowledge sharing, and communication. | Principles require operational interpretation and independent oversight. | Supports stop rules, public limitations, and avoidance of misleading claims. |
| [Metzinger 2021](https://doi.org/10.1142/S270507852150003X) | Peer-reviewed normative argument | Argues for a moratorium on research aimed at synthetic phenomenology because of suffering risk. | The scope and feasibility of the proposed moratorium are contestable. | Theta should study indicators, minimize aversive exposure, and avoid an objective of creating suffering. |

## Bottom line

The corpus supports studying theory-linked functional properties in artificial systems.
It does not supply a validated consciousness test. Positive Theta results can establish
at most B indicators and, where implementation and causal intervention are verified,
C indicators. No row in this table licenses a P claim.
