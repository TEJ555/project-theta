# NVIDIA GPT OSS V10 multi-body reliability study 02

## Status

Completed and audited on 2 October 2026. The replacement cohort passed all 11 frozen
progression rules. This is a development reliability result, not a confirmatory test of
consciousness.

## Why this study was run

V9.1 showed that the model understood the neutral response interface but did not meet
the frozen regulation threshold on its single development seed. V10 removed the
single-seed continuation decision and tested the full system across six fixed seeds and
24 independently mapped synthetic body families.

Each family used new opaque actuator names and a separately balanced hidden mapping.
The model calibrated the actuators, completed no-feedback mapping checks, followed a
changing neutral response interface, regulated the original actuators and transferred
the learned relationship to fresh aliases.

## Execution record

All six fixed seeds completed. Every run contained 176 decisions, giving 1,056 model
calls in total. All 1,056 provider response identifiers were present and unique, and
every call reported the required model `openai/gpt-oss-20b`. There were no shortened
denominators, invalid actions, welfare stops or model mismatches.

Study 01 remains preserved separately as an infrastructure failure after 21 calls. It
contributed no outcome evidence. Study 02 restarted the entire cohort from trial zero.

The hierarchical seed-and-family bootstrap interval for pooled regulation was 0.805 to
0.904. Trials were not treated as independent people or biological subjects.

## Dual-analysis check

The analyzer frozen at launch and the later hardened integrity analyzer agreed exactly
on every shared aggregate and progression decision. The hardened analyzer independently
recomputed outcomes from raw trial rows and additionally checked one completion per
seed, exact step and call counts, non-null metrics, provider identifier alignment and
exact model identity.

## What the result supports

The full model-and-wrapper system repeatedly learned and used several private synthetic
body mappings. It handled changing response codes, retained mappings without evaluation
feedback and transferred control across explicitly supplied alias relationships.

The result is stronger than a one-seed demonstration. It is also narrower than a claim
about an intrinsic model mechanism. The full condition combines current private state,
raw calibration memory, a wrapper-generated association summary, an explicit transfer
bridge and the model's action selection. V10 does not identify which component caused
the performance.

## What the result does not support

V10 does not show that the model felt the signal or possessed awareness, sentience,
suffering or phenomenal consciousness. A non-conscious controller can solve this task.
It does not validate Project Theta as a consciousness detector and does not establish
generalisation to other models, prompts, providers or body designs.

## Frozen next decision

Because every progression rule passed, the pre-outcome decision tree unlocks a new
multi-condition mechanism cohort. That cohort must separate accurate current
interoception, the association summary, raw calibration memory, the transfer bridge and
explicit mapping competence before any stronger computational interpretation is made.
