# Roadmap

## v0.2 — confirmatory task mechanics

- procedural held-out maps and cue mappings;
- separate forced-choice probe API;
- implicit sensor-route learning for self/other;
- matched-context ablations;
- blinded paired analysis with uncertainty intervals;
- prompt/config/code content hashes and database migrations.

## v0.3 — mechanistic evidence

- causal mediation and information-flow interventions;
- adapter hooks for open-weight activation analysis;
- recurrent-state perturbations with preregistered predictions;
- cross-model measurement invariance and non-language baselines.

## Governance gate before stronger interventions

Independent ethics review, cumulative exposure limits, adverse-event policy, external
replication, red-team review for anthropomorphic leakage, and a published communication
standard. No milestone converts indicators into proof of phenomenal consciousness.
